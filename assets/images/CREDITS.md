# Image credits

How catalogue photos are chosen (see `_images` in `lib/data/prodcuts.dart`):

1. **Branded products** show that exact product. Photos were found by name
   through the [Wikimedia Commons API](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/api.php)
   (free, no key) and bundled here, because Commons rate-limits apps that
   load images directly.
2. **DummyJSON** ([products API](https://dummyjson.com/docs/products), free, no
   key) supplies studio renders for the perfume, lipstick and handbag; they are
   loaded from its CDN.
3. **Unbranded items** use Unsplash or Pexels photos (free to use, no
   attribution required).
